Every water damage restoration job starts with extraction, and no other early step matters more. Quick removal of standing water means less harm to your flooring, your belongings, the building's systems and the structure itself. Left standing, water keeps moving deeper by the hour, wicking into drywall, soaking carpet pad, seeping under flooring and saturating the framing. Across Cranston, Cranston Restoration handles emergency extraction with industrial portable and truck-mounted machines that can remove hundreds of gallons a minute.

Few water removal systems match the power of a truck-mounted extractor, which draws water from structural materials, carpeting and hard flooring far better than rental or consumer machines. Where the truck hoses will not reach, we bring in submersible pumps, strong portable extractors and specialty tools made for water caught between floor layers, inside wall cavities or in tight spaces.

There is more to a professional extraction than getting rid of the water in plain sight. Our IICRC-certified technicians use moisture detection equipment to identify water that has migrated into hidden areas: behind baseboards, inside wall cavities, under cabinets and beneath flooring materials. Before drying starts, we use specialty tools to pull as much moisture as we can out of those hidden spots, so structural drying takes far less time and equipment and secondary mold is less likely.

Water Extraction Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Early leak signs show up near appliances, toilets and sink cabinets, so check those spots on a schedule
  • Put together a simple kit with a bucket, some old towels and the phone number of your restoration company
  • A sump pump that has not been checked may fail in a storm, so test it ahead of wet weather
  • A clearly labeled main water valve, in a spot everyone knows, saves time in an emergency
  • Raise anything you store in the basement a few inches above the floor
  • Maintain appliance supply lines and replace hoses that show wear or age

Signs You Need Water Extraction in Cranston

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water entering through a flooded basement stairwell or window well
  • Ongoing leakage from the plumbing, the air conditioning or an appliance
  • Water sitting on a floor anywhere in the building, no matter where it came from
  • Moisture appearing along the bottom of walls or at cabinet toe kicks
  • Water accumulating after a fire suppression system discharges
  • Laminate or wood flooring that feels spongy and pushes up water at the seams
  • Visible water where your furnace, water heater or crawl space access is
